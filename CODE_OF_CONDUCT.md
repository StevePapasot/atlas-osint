# Code of conduct

ATLAS is built by and for people doing careful, lawful research. We expect the same care in how we treat each other.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give constructive feedback on work rather than people, and
credit others' contributions.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make sexualised, discriminatory or violent remarks; publish anyone's
private information without their explicit permission (this includes using ATLAS output against project members);
or keep up behaviour after being asked to stop.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when representing the project
elsewhere.

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainer privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, and warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participants who
break this code.

Requests to use ATLAS for activities its [acceptable-use policy](docs/ACCEPTABLE_USE.md) forbids — such as locating,
monitoring or harassing a person — will be closed, and accounts may be blocked.
